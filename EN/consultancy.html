How does it work?

Analysis

We map out the question: what is going on, what is already known or has been tried, and what is the concrete goal? This prevents superficial advice.

Plan

Based on the analysis we propose something concrete: a lecture, advisory plan or research design, depending on the question and scope.

Execution

Where desired we support the execution, so it does not stay with intentions but is actually turned into results.

Engagements are always tailor-made. Form, scope and duration are adapted to the specific question, from a single lecture to a longer research or advisory project.
